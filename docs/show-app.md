# The Show app (Focus)

The app has two modes. The **full pages** (Decks, Lighting, Projection, Visuals, Stage) are for setting up and for detail; **Focus** is this page, the simple, phone-first view for running the night. Every full page's top bar (and the phone tab bar) has a **Focus** link. (It was called Live until October 2026; that clashed with the System pill's Sim / Live, where Live means the real decks are connected.) In Focus, **Full controls** (top right) opens the full page for the screen you're on. The Launchpad is on Focus's Projection screen, and its back button returns to wherever you came from. The **Launchpad** (`/pad.html`, from the Visuals page) is the visuals played as pads.

One page for running a night: the lights and the visuals, each on its own screen (the decks, mapping and stage are on their full pages), with the controls laid out round the picture rather than in tabs. It's served by the `visuals` service: **`http://sektor5.local:8110/show.html`** (on a phone, use the brain's IP). Code: [`brain/visuals/web/show.html`](../brain/visuals/web/show.html).

It drives the rig through the existing APIs, so nothing on the servers changes and every other page keeps working alongside it:

| Screen | Talks to |
|---|---|
| Lights | showbrain `:8090` (`/api/state`, `/api/cmd`) |
| Visuals | visuals `:8110` (this service); the projectors' level from projector `:8100` (`/api/layout`); the track that's playing from deckdash `:8080` (`/api/state`, `/api/art/N`) |

Addresses go through `S5AUTH.url`, so the page works on the rig's network and over HTTPS alike. POSTs go through the shared PIN check; a refused one says why in a toast instead of failing silently.

## The screens

**The top strip** is the same on both screens (and pinned under the header on phones):
- **Now playing:** the track the show is following (showbrain's live deck, else whichever is on air), its artwork, artist and key, the tempo, the time left and a line with its drops, and what's loaded and waiting on the other deck (Up next).
- **The phase:** where the show is in the track, from the lights' scene engine. That's the scene in words (Groove, Building, Here it comes, Drop, Break…), the bar, how far to the drop (or how far into it, or how built), the movement the lights are playing, and the beat.
- **Which screen you're in:** Lights, or Projection with what you're playing with (Focus, or a surface's name). "held" shows when a scene is latched.

- **Lights.** Scene, **Movement** (how the lights play off each other across the stage: Auto, or latch Together, Alternate, Call & answer, Bounce, Chase, Out or In; see [show-engine.md](show-engine.md#interplay-the-lights-play-off-each-other)), the hits (Strobe, Blinder, Flash, Blackout), a colour ring with **Follow the music** in the middle, and the master level.
- **Projection.** The picture is the projector's own output, as mapped (its layout from the Projection editor): every surface playing what it plays, or the Focus look full frame if the projector has no surfaces yet. Under it, **what you're playing with**: **Focus** (the look every surface set to Focus plays, the one Shuffle moves through), then one chip per surface (with what it shows), and a projector picker when there's more than one. Tap a chip, or the surface on the picture, to play with it: it's outlined on the picture (all the Focus surfaces get a dashed outline while Focus is picked), named on the picture's caption, and the Look and Presets panels say "for" it.
  - With a surface picked, tapping a look gives that surface the look as its own (**↩ Focus** at the top of the list hands it back), presets and Randomise set its preset, and Next look gives it another look from the theme.
  - Energy, Intensity, Shuffle, Changes and Words only play the Focus look, so they step back (dimmed, with a note) while a surface plays a look of its own. Play the knobs plays every surface.
  - Changes save to the layout straight away, on a fresh copy of it, so a change made in the Projection editor meanwhile isn't undone. **Full controls** opens the Projection editor; the full Visuals page (Setup → Design looks) is for working on one sketch in detail.
  - The preview (with an On air / Frozen / Reconnecting badge top left and the bar's four beats top right, with the bar number in the phrase beside them: 1 to 4, tap it to count to 8, 16 or 2, one setting shared with the Visuals page) with Freeze and Next look, an **Intensity** fader (calm to full on, played live: it glides, and only moves how strongly the picture reacts, the A / B / C drive amounts and amplitudes like kick punch and glow, never speeds, counts or the look itself; a sketch can opt a setting in or out with `"intensity": true/false`; see `VJ.intensityValues`), and the projectors' output level. On the left, the **look**: the themes, the looks in the one you pick (tap one to mix to it), and **Shuffle** (on/off, every 1–32 bars, Skip, and when the next change lands). The theme you pick is the one Shuffle plays from. On the right, energy (Calm / Groove / Drop), **Play the knobs** (the look's settings ride the song: Subtle, Lively or Wild, or off; see [visuals-live.md](visuals-live.md#play-the-knobs)), the presets, **Randomise** (a random preset for this look, **R**), and **Words** for looks that draw type. On a phone the picture stays pinned at the top while everything else scrolls under it; the Intensity fader and energy come straight after. The **Audio monitor** (Setup → Audio monitor, shared with the Visuals page's waveform button and the V key) is the track's waveform in its own panel under the picture, for the person running the visuals: never on the projector. Scroll, pinch or −/+ to zoom from 1 to 16 bars. On phones Lights' fixture strips are pinned the same way. **Changes** (under Randomise, in both views) sets how one look hands over to the next: Auto, Cut, Fade, **Pick** (tick the transitions you like; each change picks one of them at random, never the same twice running) or any other transition, landing now, on the beat, on the bar or on the drop (the visuals service's `/api/transition`).

The **Autopilot** pill at the top says what the room is doing that the music didn't ask for (a latched scene, a locked colour, frozen visuals…); tap it to hand everything back. **Setup** (the gear) holds Appearance (dark, light or match the device), the Audio monitor, and links to the full pages for everything done before the night.

## Keys

Space flash · S / W strobe, blinder (hold) · B blackout · 0–4 scenes · D drop now · Z / X / C visuals calm, groove, drop · N next look · R randomise · L Shuffle on/off · F freeze · ? Setup and the key list · Esc closes Setup. Esc no longer hands everything back to autopilot: it's the key you reach for to get out of something, and it shouldn't also drop a blackout mid-set. Use the pill, which says **Reset** while anything is overridden.

## Look

The page has its own design system (it opts out of the shared theme with `<html data-s5-own-theme>`, which is what keeps its light mode working; the lock sits in its header, in `[data-s5lock]`), not the charcoal-and-hairline one in [design.md](design.md): near-black and flat, zinc neutrals with 1 px borders, sentence case, a light mode. The tokens are at the top of the stylesheet. One accent, Sektor orange, and it only means live or on: the look, energy or preset that's playing, Shuffle running, a held hit. A selected choice (a segment, a chip, a theme) is white; blackout is red. Radius 8 / 12 / 16; controls 32–40 px on desktop and at least 44 px on phones. It uses system fonts, so nothing is fetched from the web (the brain is often offline at a gig). The screen stays awake while the page is open (Wake Lock), and hover styles are off on touch screens so a tapped tile doesn't stay lit. The pictures (preview, mapping, stage) stay dark in both themes. On a laptop or desktop every screen fits the window; on a phone the picture comes first and the controls stack under it, at 40 px or more to tap.

## Not here yet

Focus has two screens, Lights and Projection; the decks (automix, the music, waveforms), mapping surfaces (corners, masks) and the stage are on their full pages (Setup, or **Full controls** from the screen you're on). Also still only on the full pages: every sketch setting, saving presets, transition settings, Shuffle's per-look tick-outs and queueing a look for next, the Launchpad's effects and quantised launch; build lengths other than 4 bars, hold / cancel build, skip or mark a drop, strobe rate, motion speed, tap tempo, Mixer React, per-fixture levels; kick align and tempo ramps; projector output latency and resolution; the simulation and System controls.
