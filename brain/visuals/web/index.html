How the sliders work
the value how far it may move (drag the ▲ under it) automation, moving it now

Drag the slider to set the value and the ▲ ends to narrow its range. Shift-drag for fine, double-click to reset, wheel to nudge. A automates the selected one. On a phone or tablet: swipe up and down to scroll, drag sideways along a slider to move its value (from where it is), tap to select, and use Edit range in the selected one's row to move the ▲ ends.

Calm / Groove / Drop are the same dice at three energies: they aim the speeds, counts and reaction amounts low, middle or high. They and Randomise stay inside every range, and ranges and automation are saved with a preset. ? lists the keys.

Transitions

How the projector hands over when the sketch changes (or a preset loads), in time with the music. Auto picks a type that suits the song section: a flash, stutter or zoom into a drop, zooms and swirls through a build, slow dissolves in a breakdown, wipes and irises in the groove.
