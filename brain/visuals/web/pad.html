Launch on decides when a pad takes effect: now, or on the next beat or bar, so a change always lands in time however sloppily you hit it. A queued pad fills up as the bar comes round; tap it again to cancel. Effects and Hold pads always act at once — they are played, not launched.

On a laptop: Q W E energy, Z sweep up, X sweep down, C strobe, V flash, B flip, 1–8 presets, space freeze, esc panic, S shuffle, N skip. Hold the key for the held ones.
